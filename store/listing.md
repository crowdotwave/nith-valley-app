## Full description (both stores)

For clients of Nith Valley Animal Hospital in New Hamburg, Ontario.

Order your pet's food and medication, keep track of what is coming due, and
collect points every visit.

REFILLS WITHOUT THE PHONE CALL
Send a food or medication request in a few taps and follow it until it is
ready to pick up.

KNOW BEFORE YOU RUN OUT
Tell the app what your pet eats and takes, and it works out when you will run
low and reminds you in time.

WHAT IS COMING DUE
See vaccinations and check-ups our team has on file for each of your animals.

YOUR PET'S FILE
Diet, medications, vaccinations and a photo, for every animal in your
household, shared with everyone in it.

POINTS
Collect points at checkout and see what you can claim.

BOOK AND REACH US
Book through our online scheduling, call, text or find us.

Sign in with the email address the clinic has on file for you. There is no
password.
