## If it is published from a personal developer account

The store pages will show the developer's own name while the app carries the
practice's name and logo. Reviewers may ask for proof that the practice has
agreed. Before submitting, get a short signed letter or email from the
practice saying the developer may publish the app under its name and logo, and
attach it in App Store Connect (App Review Information, Attachment). Add this
line to the notes:

> Published by **TO CONFIRM (developer name)** on behalf of Nith Valley Animal
> Hospital, with its written permission (attached).

## App Review Information: Notes

This app is for existing clients of Nith Valley Animal Hospital, a veterinary
practice in New Hamburg, Ontario. Clients use it to request food and
medication refills, track those requests, keep their animals' diet and
medication details, see vaccinations and check-ups the practice has on file,
send photos to the practice, and collect loyalty points. Practice staff use
the same app to work the requests.

Sign-in is by a one-time email link, with no password, using the email
address the practice holds for the client, so a reviewer cannot sign in with
a real account.

To review the full app, tap **TO CONFIRM (link wording)** on the sign-in
screen. It opens a complete sample practice with invented clients and animals.
Every feature works there, and nothing is sent to the practice. A bar at the
top of every screen marks it as sample data.

"Book an appointment" opens the practice's own online scheduling, run by
Covetrus, its practice management provider, in an in-app browser sheet.

Push notifications remind clients when food or medication is about to run
out and when a request is ready.

## Sign-in required?

Apple: "Sign-in required" is checked, and the note above explains the
sample-data route instead of a demo account.
