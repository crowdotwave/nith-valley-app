This policy explains what the Nith Valley Animal Hospital app collects, why,
where it is kept, and what you can do about it. It covers the app on iPhone
and Android and the same app on the web. It does not cover our clinic records,
which are kept in our practice management system under our existing privacy
practices.

## What we collect

**Your account.** Your email address, which is how you sign in, and
optionally your name and phone number. Your email must match the one we hold
for you, so we can connect your app account to your client file.

**Your household.** Who shares your animals with you, so that everyone in a
household sees the same pets and requests.

**Your animals.** Name, species, breed, sex, date of birth and a photo if you
add one. Their diet and medications: product names, amounts, and when you
last bought or filled them, which is how the app works out when you will run
low.

**Care dates.** Vaccination and reminder dates. Our staff enter these from our
clinic records; the app never works them out on its own.

**Requests.** Food and medication refill requests, any notes you add, and
their status as our staff work on them.

**Photos you send us.** Pictures you submit for our social media and website,
with a record of the permission you gave and the wording you agreed to.

**Loyalty points.** Points our staff award at checkout and the rewards you
claim.

**Notifications.** If you allow notifications, a device address that lets us
send reminders to your phone. It identifies the app on your phone, not you.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, advertising identifiers, or
usage analytics, and the app contains no advertising or tracking.

## Why we use it

Only to run the app for you: to show your animals and what is coming due, to
receive and fill your requests, to send reminders, to keep your points, and to
post photos you have given us permission to post. We do not sell your
information or use it for advertising.

## Who can see it

People in your household see your household's animals, requests and points.
Our staff see what they need to serve you. Nobody else does.

We use these service providers to run the app. Each handles your information
only to provide its service to us:

- **Supabase**, which stores the app's data in its Canada (Central) region.
- **TO CONFIRM (email provider)**, which sends sign-in emails.
- **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service**,
  which deliver notifications to your phone if you allow them.

Booking an appointment opens our scheduling provider's website (Covetrus).
What you enter there is covered by their privacy policy, not this one.

## Photos for social media

We only post a photo you have sent with your permission. You can ask us to
take a photo down at any time. We will remove it from the app and from our own
accounts; we cannot recall copies that other people have already shared.

## How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. When you delete your account:
**TO CONFIRM (what is deleted, what is kept, and for how long; for example,

## Your choices

- **See or correct your information** in the app, or ask us.
- **Delete your account** in the app under **TO CONFIRM (menu path)**, or by
  asking us at the contact above.
- **Turn notifications off** at any time in your phone's settings.
- **Withdraw permission for a photo** by asking us.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle a privacy concern, you can
contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

## Children

The app is for pet owners and is not directed at children.

## Security

Information travels encrypted between the app and our servers. Access is
limited by account, so a household can only reach its own records.

## Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above, and tell
you in the app if the change is significant.
